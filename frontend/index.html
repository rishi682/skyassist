Frequently Asked Questions

What compensation do I get for flight delays?

Under 3 hours: ₹500 meal voucher
3-5 hours: Meal voucher + lounge access
Over 5 hours: Meal voucher + hotel (delayed hours only)

Can I get a refund for a cancelled flight?

Yes! For airline-caused cancellations, you can choose between a free rebooking on the next available flight or a full refund within 7 business days.

Do loyalty tiers get extra benefits?

Gold and Platinum members get priority rebooking on available seats, but compensation follows the standard policy.

When do I need to talk to a human agent?

For compensation beyond policy, legal threats, or special cases where I can't fully help, I'll escalate you to our specialist team immediately.
